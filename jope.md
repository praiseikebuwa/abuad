Here is a curated list of high-impact features, screens, and pages tailored specifically for the **ABUAD Campus Super-App**, categorized by student need:

---

### 1. 🎓 Academic & Exam Tools
* **Exam Countdown & Study Planner (`.exam`)**
  * A visual countdown timer for GST tests, mid-semester assessments, and final exams.
  * Includes a built-in **Pomodoro study timer** with study checklists organized by course code.
* **CGPA Target & Semester Simulator (`.target` / `.cgpa`)**
  * An interactive simulator where students select their target graduating honor (e.g. *First Class 4.50+* or *2:1*) and it calculates the exact letter grades they need in each registered course to hit that goal.
* **CBT Practice & Past Questions Hub (`.cbt` / `.practice`)**
  * Interactive mock exam practice for university-wide courses (e.g. GST 111, GST 112, GST 211, MTH 101, CHM 101).
  * Instant scoring, timers, review explanations, and leaderboards.
* **75% Lecture Attendance Tracker (`.attendance`)**
  * Tracks mandatory lecture and chapel attendance against university thresholds, with warning alerts if a student is at risk of falling below exam eligibility.

---

### 2. 🍔 Campus Life, Food & Hostels
* **Campus Food & Cafeteria Hub (`.food` / `.buka`)**
  * Menus, daily specials, opening hours, and pre-ordering for campus cafeterias, tuck shops, and student eateries across colleges and hostels.
* **Hostel Maintenance & Roommate Hub (`.hostel`)**
  * Direct reporting of room maintenance issues (e.g., plumbing, electrical, carpentry) to hall wardens with photo attachments and status tracking.
  * Roommate compatibility matching for the start of new sessions.
* **Campus Shuttle & Travel Board (`.shuttle` / `.ride`)**
  * Shuttle route timetables and stops (Colleges, Hostels, Sports Complex, Talent Discovery Centre, Teaching Hospital).
  * "Going to Town / Airport" carpool board for students traveling home for breaks together to split fares.

---

### 3. 💰 Student Finance & Micro-Economy
* **Campus Wallet & Split Bills (`.wallet` / `.split`)**
  * Virtual student wallet for instant transfers via @handle or QR code.
  * Group bill splitter for hostel food deliveries, projects, and birthday contributions.
* **Student Gigs & Freelance Board (`.gigs` / `.services`)**
  * Dedicated directory for student entrepreneurs to offer services on campus: hair styling/barbing, graphic design, photography, laptop repairs, peer tutoring, and event MCing.

---

### 4. 🏆 Social, Sports & Campus Culture
* **Campus Polls & Hall Debates (`.polls`)**
  * Real-time polls on campus topics (e.g., "Best Cafeteria Food", "Faculty Week Voting", "Hall Football Predictions").
* **Inter-Hall Sports & VC Cup Center (`.sports` / `.league`)**
  * Live fixtures, results, standings, top scorers, and match commentary for inter-hall football, basketball, volleyball, and athletics.
* **Audio Study Lounges & Course Circles (`.studygroup`)**
  * Drop-in voice and text study rooms created around course codes (e.g., `#ENG202-Revision`, `#LAW101-Discussion`).

---

### 5. 🚨 Health, Safety & Emergency
* **Campus SOS / Emergency Alert (`.sos`)**
  * One-tap emergency button that transmits the student's live location to the Campus Security Unit (CSO) and the ABUAD Teaching Hospital emergency line.
* **Health Centre & Pharmacy Queue (`.clinic`)**
  * Check clinic consultation hours, book appointments, and see live queue numbers at the university health centre.
